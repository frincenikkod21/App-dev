# 🎬 Movie Review: Doctor Strange (2016)

This is an incredible Marvel Cinematic Universe film that explores the mystic arts and alternate dimensions. It follows the journey of a brilliant but arrogant neurosurgeon who discovers magic after a life-altering accident.

## ✨ Key Information
 * **Director:** Scott Derrickson
 * **Main Actor:** Benedict Cumberbatch (as Dr. Stephen Strange)
 * **Release Year:** 2016
 * **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy

## 🧙‍♂️ Top 3 Best Characters
 1. **Dr. Stephen Strange** – The master of the mystic arts.
 2. **The Ancient One** – The wise Sorcerer Supreme and mentor.
 3. **Wong** – The reliable librarian and master protector of ancient relics.

> "Doctor Strange, you think you know how the world works? You think that this material universe is all there is? What is real? What mysteries lie beyond the reach of your senses?" — *The Ancient One*

---
 ### 🔮 Why You Should Watch It
 * **Mind-Bending Visuals:** The kaleidoscope-like shifting cities and mirror dimensions are breathtaking.
 * **Character Growth:** It features an excellent redemption arc from a selfish doctor to a selfless hero.
 * **Unique Magic System:** Unlike typical superhero films, the combat relies on martial arts mixed with reality-warping sorcery.
